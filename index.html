Over 30 Years of Ministry in Uganda

New Testament Evangelical Church (NTEC) was founded over three decades ago in Kitintale, Kampala, with a vision to preach the whole Bible and New Testament realities to Uganda and beyond.

Under the faithful leadership of Bishop Mutebi, the church has grown to include multiple ministries, cells across Kampala and Uganda, a vibrant worship team, and a Bible school for training pastors.

We believe in the power of the Holy Spirit, the authority of Scripture, and the commission to make disciples of all nations.

Our Cells

NTEC has home fellowship cells in different areas of Kampala and across Uganda. These cells meet during the week for Bible study, prayer, and fellowship.

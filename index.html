A Living Church in the Heart of Kampala

For over 30 years, New Testament Evangelical Church has been a beacon of hope and transformation in Kampala. We're not just a religious institution—we're a vibrant community where lives are changed through the power of the Gospel.

Our mission is simple: to preach Christ crucified and risen, and to see His transforming power at work in every person who walks through our doors.
